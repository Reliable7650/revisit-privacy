Revisit

A second look at what you saved.

Revisit brings one collected bookmark back to the top of X Home. Unseen posts come first; the posts you have seen least get the next turn.

Browse your X bookmarks normally to collect the posts the page loads. Open the original post and its replies, or remove and restore a bookmark from its Home card. Displayed engagement counts are the last collected snapshot.

Agree once to local collection, then use Revisit directly—even after restarting Chrome. No new password, extra Revisit account, paid API, ads or analytics.

Version 0.3.0 encrypts local records with a device-generated key stored in this Chrome profile. The key and data stay together; this is not independent-password protection against reading or copying the entire profile. An older 0.2.0 store may need its old password once to migrate existing posts and display counts.
