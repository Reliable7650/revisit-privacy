Revisit

A second look at what you saved.

Revisit brings one collected bookmark back to the top of X Home. Unseen posts come first; the posts you have seen least get the next turn.

Browse your X bookmarks normally to collect the posts the page loads. Open the original post and its replies, or remove and restore a bookmark from its Home card. Displayed engagement counts are the last collected snapshot.

After your explicit agreement, your collected posts and display history are encrypted in this Chrome profile. Unlock once after restarting Chrome. No extra Revisit account, paid API, ads or analytics.

Pre-release. This build is not yet ready for Chrome Web Store submission. Local consent, password protection and browser checks are implemented. X integration permission and Chrome Web Store submission remain pending. There is no Chrome Web Store installation link yet.
