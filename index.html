Revisit

A second look at what you saved.

Install from the Chrome Web Store · 从 Chrome 商店安装

Install Revisit once from the Chrome Web Store. Chrome updates that installation in place and keeps its saved bookmarks and loading progress in the same profile. 请通过 Chrome 商店安装一次，后续升级会保留本机书签和加载进度。

Revisit automatically brings one collected X bookmark to X Home, or one collected Mastodon bookmark to m.cmx.im Home. No platform selector is needed. Each platform and account has its own bookmark library and display counts. Unseen posts come first; the posts you have seen least get the next turn.

On your first use of each platform, open its native bookmarks and agree to automatic loading in Revisit. Opening only the first page includes only recent saves. Only loaded bookmarks can be resurfaced, and the collected count does not mean your entire library has loaded.

Open the original post and its replies, or remove and restore a bookmark from its Home card. Displayed engagement counts are the last collected snapshot. X also offers an optional background load-more control with separate agreement and a Stop action. Both sites also support automatic loading directly from their bookmark pages.

Agree once to local collection, then use Revisit directly—even after restarting Chrome. No new password, extra Revisit account, paid API, ads or analytics.

Revisit encrypts local records with a device-generated key stored in this Chrome profile. The key and data stay together; this is not independent-password protection against reading or copying the entire profile. An older 0.2.0 store may need its old password once to migrate existing posts and display counts.
