Revisit

A second look at what you saved.

Revisit automatically brings one collected X bookmark to X Home, or one collected Mastodon bookmark to m.cmx.im Home. No platform selector is needed. Each platform and account has its own bookmark library and display counts. Unseen posts come first; the posts you have seen least get the next turn.

On your first use of each platform, open its native bookmarks and keep scrolling down while older batches load. Opening only the first page includes only recent saves. Only loaded bookmarks can be resurfaced, and the collected count does not mean your entire library has loaded.

Open the original post and its replies, or remove and restore a bookmark from its Home card. Displayed engagement counts are the last collected snapshot. X also offers an optional background load-more control with separate agreement and a Stop action. Mastodon uses the native bookmark page; it has no separate authorization or active batch reader.

Agree once to local collection, then use Revisit directly—even after restarting Chrome. No new password, extra Revisit account, paid API, ads or analytics.

Version 0.4.2 encrypts local records with a device-generated key stored in this Chrome profile. The key and data stay together; this is not independent-password protection against reading or copying the entire profile. An older 0.2.0 store may need its old password once to migrate existing posts and display counts.

Pre-release 0.4.2. Chrome Web Store submission and review are pending. This website provides privacy and support information; there is no Chrome Web Store installation link yet.
