Getting started

Open Revisit, read the collection notice, choose a local password and select Agree and protect my bookmarks. Then sign in to X, open its Bookmarks view and browse normally. Return to X Home to see a collected post. Revisit does not fetch an entire library automatically.

A bookmark or number is missing

Only posts loaded during normal Bookmarks browsing enter the local pool. Revisit's Home totals are collected snapshots. Revisit the bookmark page to update them. Changes to X can affect collection or actions.

Local password and locking

Unlock once after restarting Chrome. Lock Revisit to stop collection and hide stored content. The local password cannot be recovered; if it is lost, Clear local data resets the local store without removing bookmarks on X.

Removing local data

Use Clear local data in the popup. This erases the encrypted store, display history, consent and local password setup. It does not delete bookmarks on X. Close the Bookmarks page to avoid immediately collecting its posts again. Uninstalling also removes local extension data. The Home display switch only hides the Home card.

Reporting a problem

Include your Chrome version, Revisit version and the steps that caused the problem. Remove private content from any screenshot. Do not send passwords, cookies, authorization headers or session tokens.
