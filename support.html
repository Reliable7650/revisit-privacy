Getting started

In Revisit 0.3.0, open the popup, read the collection notice and explicitly agree. Do not create a new password: there is no password setup for a fresh installation. Then sign in to X, open its Bookmarks view and browse normally. Return to Home to see a collected post. Revisit does not fetch an entire library automatically.

A bookmark or number is missing

Only posts loaded during normal Bookmarks browsing enter the local pool. Revisit's Home totals are collected snapshots. Revisit the bookmark page to update them. Changes to X can affect collection or actions.

No routine password

After first agreement, Revisit uses its local device key automatically, including after a Chrome restart. The non-extractable key and encrypted records are in the same profile. This is not the same protection as a separate password and does not protect against someone who can read or copy the whole profile.

Upgrading a 0.2.0 store

A valid old unlocked session can migrate saved posts and counts automatically. Otherwise enter the old local password once for migration; do not choose a new password. After migration, normal use does not require that password. If it is forgotten and no valid session can open the old store, you can clear local data and recollect from X. Do not email the old password to support.

Removing local data

Clear local data removes encrypted content, display history, the device key and consent, without deleting bookmarks on X. Collection remains off until you explicitly agree again, with no new-password step. Uninstalling removes local extension data. The Home switch only hides the card; close Bookmarks or disable the extension to stop collection after agreement.

Reporting a problem

Include your Chrome version, Revisit version and the steps that caused the problem. Remove private content from any screenshot. Do not send passwords, cookies, authorization headers or session tokens.
